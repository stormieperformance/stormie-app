How to Play

  • Goal: be the last jelly wobbling. 3 rivals, 1 arena.
  • Each turn, click a tile within 2 squares of you to jump there.
  • 16 special tiles, 5 kinds: Weapon tiles explode the instant you land on them, Status tiles buff you or debuff nearby rivals, Teleport flings you somewhere random, Hazard tiles hurt you, and Health tiles heal you.
  • Land on a weapon tile and it goes off right there on the board — a Bomb-type Blast or Handbag hits a 3×3 area around you, Laser sweeps your whole row and column, and Nuke hits the entire arena.
  • Icons drift south every turn and fall off the map, so aim for where one is heading, not where it sits now.
  • Land on the same tile as a rival and it's a Jelly Off — everyone rolls, the winner holds the tile and punches the rest away.
  • Get punched off the edge of the board and you're out.

You Win!

Every rival jelly has been wobbled out of the arena.
