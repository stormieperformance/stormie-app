How to Play

  • Goal: be the last jelly with HP remaining. 3 rivals, 1 arena.
  • All four jellies choose a tile within 2 squares and jump simultaneously each turn.
  • Icons shift one row south right before everyone lands, so aim one key below the item you actually want.
  • Handbag isn't instant: it grants 3 future turns of automatic close-range swings at whoever's adjacent.
  • Blast does distance-based damage (hardest up close, weaker further out). Laser hits your whole row and column. Air Strike launches several random missiles. Nuke hits every unshielded rival at once. Random Drop crushes one rival you can see.
  • Land on the same tile as a rival for a Jelly Off: everyone rolls, the winner holds the key, and losers get knocked onto a nearby open tile.

You Win!

Every rival jelly has been wobbled out of the arena.
