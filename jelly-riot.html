Stormie Games presentsJelly Riot

Four jellies. One arena. Everybody jumps at once.

  • Last jelly wobbling wins. Tap a glowing key within two squares. All four jellies jump at the same time.
  • The icons slide down one row just before you land, so aim one key below the item you want.
  • Weapons: Blast hurts most up close, Laser hits your row and column, Air Strike rains missiles, Nuke hits every unshielded rival, Random Drop crushes someone.
  • Handbag swings at anyone next to you for three turns. Shields block one hit. Mines hurt you.
  • Land on the same key as a rival for a Jelly Off. Highest roll keeps the key.
Play upright or sideways. Just turn your phone.
